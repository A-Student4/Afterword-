How HoldsUp decides what counts as a match

Each finding is scored on how much of your search it explains, weighting rare words more than common ones. A match is strong when it covers at least % of your search or contains a known name for the finding, and weak from %. Below that, HoldsUp says it found nothing rather than showing a poor guess. Lowering these thresholds would show more results but more wrong ones.

Paste a paragraph from an essay or report. HoldsUp marks sentences that name or cite a finding in its collection. It does not judge sentences it does not recognise.
